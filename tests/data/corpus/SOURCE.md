# Test corpus — Project Gutenberg (illustrated, multi-level TOC)

`tests/data/corpus/` holds **real** public-domain books used by the end-to-end
tests (`tests/test_corpus.py`). They are English works, downloaded from
[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) in the **`.epub.images`** edition,
chosen because they mix **illustrations** with a **multi-level table of
contents** — exactly what the translation pipeline must preserve.

`MANIFEST.json` is the machine-readable catalogue (title, author, image count,
`<img>` tags, TOC depth, size); it is regenerated by
`python tools/fetch_corpus_fixtures.py`.
